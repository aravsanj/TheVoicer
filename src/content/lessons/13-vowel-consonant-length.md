## Sound Length Is Not Fixed

In English, the same sound can be held for a longer or shorter time depending on where it appears and what comes after it. This variation in **length** is one of the things that gives natural English its characteristic rhythm.

---

## Vowel Length: Voiced vs. Voiceless Consonants

A vowel sound is held **longer** before a **voiced** consonant than before a **voiceless** consonant.

Compare these pairs — the vowel in each word is the same sound, but it's held for different lengths of time:

| Shorter vowel (before voiceless) | Longer vowel (before voiced) |
|----------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **cap** /kæp/                    | **cab** /kæːb/               |
| **bet** /bɛt/                    | **bed** /bɛːd/               |
| **rice** /raɪs/                  | **rise** /raɪːz/             |
| **back** /bæk/                   | **bag** /bæːɡ/               |
| **leaf** /liːf/                  | **leave** /liːːv/            |

The vowel in **cab** is noticeably longer than in **cap**, even though both use the /æ/ sound. Native speakers rely on this length difference — not just the final consonant — to distinguish the two words.

---

## Vowel Length: Sentence Position

A vowel sound is held **longer** when it ends a word that appears at the **end of a sentence**.

| Mid-sentence (shorter)              | End of sentence (longer)        |
|-------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| *I saw her **go** quickly.*         | *I watched her **go**.*         |
| *The answer is **no**, actually.*   | *The answer is **no**.*         |
| *She said **yes** right away.*      | *She finally said **yes**.*     |

This lengthening at the end of a sentence is connected to falling intonation. The voice slows and drops, which naturally extends the final sound.

---

## Consonant Length: Final Position

Final consonant sounds follow their own length pattern.

**Voiceless final consonants** (/f/, /s/, /ʃ/, /θ/, /tʃ/) are held **longer** than voiced final consonants (/v/, /z/, /ʒ/, /ð/, /dʒ/).

| Shorter (voiced final)  | Longer (voiceless final) |
|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| **leave** /liːv/        | **leaf** /liːf/          |
| **prize** /praɪz/       | **price** /praɪs/        |
| **rouge** /ruːʒ/        | **rush** /rʌʃ/           |

This may seem counterintuitive — voiced sounds typically involve more vocal activity. But the compensation works like a trade-off: the **vowel** before a voiced consonant gets longer, while the **voiced consonant itself** is shorter. The voiceless consonant, by contrast, gets more time and airflow.

---

## Stops Cannot Be Held

The length rules above apply to **continuant** sounds — sounds you can sustain. **Stops** (sounds made by fully blocking and releasing the airflow) cannot be held.

| Voiceless stops | Voiced stops |
|-----------------|--------------|
| /p/ (cap)       | /b/ (cab)    |
| /t/ (bet)       | /d/ (bed)    |
| /k/ (back)      | /ɡ/ (bag)    |

For words ending in these sounds, the length difference is perceived through the **vowel** before the stop, not through the stop itself. **cap** and **cab** are distinguished primarily because the vowel in **cab** is longer — not because you hold the final /b/ longer than the /p/.

---

## Putting It Together

| Word     | Vowel length | Consonant length | Reason                            |
|----------|--------------|------------------|------------------------------------|
| **cap**  | Short        | (stop — n/a)     | Voiceless stop follows             |
| **cab**  | Long         | (stop — n/a)     | Voiced stop follows                |
| **race** | Short        | Long /s/         | Voiceless continuant follows/ends  |
| **rays** | Long         | Short /z/        | Voiced continuant ends the word    |
| **go**   | Long         | —                | Word-final in sentence             |

Paying attention to these length patterns — even subtly — will make your speech sound more natural and help you distinguish minimal pairs when listening.
