## Suffixes and Stress: A Predictable System

Many English suffixes have a fixed relationship with stress. Learning these patterns gives you a powerful shortcut for figuring out stress in new words.

---

## Stress-Fixing Suffixes (stress falls on a specific syllable)

### Suffixes that pull stress to the syllable immediately before them

These suffixes are **stress-attracting** — the syllable right before the suffix gets primary stress.

**-tion / -sion / -cion**
| Word           | IPA                      | Stressed syllable |
|----------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| proDUCtion     | /prəˈdʌk·ʃən/            | -duc-             |
| inforMAtion    | /ˌɪn·fɚˈmeɪ·ʃən/         | -ma-              |
| compreHENsion  | /ˌkɑm·prɪˈhɛn·ʃən/       | -hen-             |
| suspiCion      | /səˈspɪʃ·ən/             | -pi-              |

**-ic / -ical**
| Word          | IPA                       | Stressed syllable |
|---------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| eLECtric      | /ɪˈlɛk·trɪk/              | -lec-             |
| ecoNOMic      | /ˌɛk·əˈnɑm·ɪk/            | -nom-             |
| hiSTORical    | /hɪˈstɔr·ɪ·kəl/           | -tor-             |
| bioLOGical    | /ˌbaɪ·əˈlɑdʒ·ɪ·kəl/       | -log-             |

**-ity / -ety**
| Word          | IPA                       | Stressed syllable |
|---------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| possiBILity   | /ˌpɑs·əˈbɪl·ɪ·tiː/         | -bil-             |
| sinCErity     | /sɪnˈsɛr·ɪ·tiː/            | -cer-             |
| maJORity      | /məˈdʒɔr·ɪ·tiː/            | -jor-             |

**-ious / -eous / -uous**

| amBIGuous      | /æmˈbɪɡ·ju·əs/           | -big-             |

## Stress-Neutral Suffixes (stress stays on the base word)

These suffixes do **not** change where stress falls. The original root keeps its stress.

**-ment, -ness, -ful, -less, -er, -ly, -ing, -ed, -s**

| Base word | IPA           | With suffix  | IPA               |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|-------------------|
| HAPpy     | /ˈhæp·iː/      | HAPpiness    | /ˈhæp·iː·nəs/      |
| CAREful   | /ˈkɛr·fəl/    | CAREfully    | /ˈkɛr·fəl·iː/      |
| MANage    | /ˈmæn·ɪdʒ/    | MANagement   | /ˈmæn·ɪdʒ·mənt/   |

## Prefixes and Stress

Most prefixes in English are **unstressed** — they don't attract stress to themselves. The stress stays on the root.

| Prefix | Word            | IPA                      | Stress on root |
|--------|-----------------|--------------------------|----------------|
| re-    | rePEAT          | /rɪˈpiːt/                | root: -peat    |
| un-    | unHAPpy         | /ʌnˈhæp·iː/               | root: -hap-    |
| dis-   | disagREE        | /ˌdɪs·əˈɡriː/            | root: -gree    |
| pre-   | prePARE         | /prɪˈpɛr/                | root: -pare    |
| mis-   | misunderSTAND   | /ˌmɪs·ʌn·dɚˈstænd/       | root: -stand   |
| over-  | overCOME        | /ˌoʊ·vɚˈkʌm/             | root: -come    |

**Exception:** When the same prefix+root pair is used as both a noun and a verb, the noun often shifts stress to the prefix. (See the next lesson on noun vs. verb stress shifts.)

## Putting It Together

When you encounter an unfamiliar word, look for a suffix you recognize and apply the rule:

1. *sophistication* → ends in **-tion** → stress on syllable before it → **-ca-** → /səˌfɪs·tɪˈkeɪ·ʃən/ ✓
2. *democratic* → ends in **-ic** → stress on syllable before it → **-crat-** → /ˌdɛm·əˈkræt·ɪk/ ✓
3. *carefully* → ends in **-ly** (neutral suffix) → stress stays on root *care* → /ˈkɛr·fəl·iː/ ✓

These patterns won't work 100% of the time, but they're accurate often enough to be worth internalizing.
